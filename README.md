# Orbit

**Build better habits and get things done, all in one beautiful app.**

Orbit is a habit and task tracker for your phone. It helps you plan your day, check off what you've done, and see how consistent you've been over time. Everything stays on your phone. There's no account to create, no sign-up, and nothing is sent to a server.

## Why Orbit?

- **Private by default.** Your habits and tasks live only on your device.
- **Simple to use.** Open the app, see your day, tap to check things off.
- **Nice to look at.** Smooth animations, frosted-glass effects, and six color themes make it feel good to use every day.
- **Keeps you motivated.** Streaks, charts, and a daily progress ring show how far you've come.

---

## What you can do

### See your whole day at a glance
The **Today** screen shows your habits and tasks for the day, plus a glowing three-ring **Orb** that fills up as you complete things. Finish everything and you get a little celebration.

You can also swipe through a week strip at the top to look back at past days or peek at the days ahead.

<!-- ![Today screen](./screenshots/today.png) -->

### Manage your tasks
Add tasks with a priority, a due date, and a tag. Filter them by **All open**, **Today**, **Upcoming**, or **Done**. You can set a reminder so you never forget something important.

<!-- ![Tasks screen](./screenshots/tasks.png) -->

### Build habits that stick
Create habits you want to do every day, or only on certain days of the week. Each habit shows your **current streak** and your **best streak**, and you can switch between two views:

- **Weekly view:** a quick row of checkboxes for the past 7 days
- **Heatmap view:** an 18-week grid (like GitHub's contribution graph) showing how consistent you've been

Want a nudge? Add a daily reminder to any habit.

<!-- ![Habits screen](./screenshots/habits.png) -->

### Watch your progress grow
The **Stats** screen turns your effort into pictures:

- **Chart:** see your completion trend over the last 7, 30, or 90 days. Drag your finger across it to check any day.
- **Calendar:** a monthly view where each day is color-coded by how much you completed. Tap any day to see exactly what you did.

<!-- ![Stats screen](./screenshots/stats.png) -->

### Make it yours
Tap your profile button to:

- Set your name (Orbit greets you with it)
- Pick from **6 themes**: 3 dark and 3 light
- **Back up** your data and restore it any time

| Dark themes | Light themes |
|---|---|
| Midnight Orbit | Lavender Pearl |
| Deep Space OLED | Pure Snow |
| Aurora Slate | Warm Sand |

## Reminders

Orbit can send you notifications for tasks (at the time you choose) and habits (every day or on specific weekdays). If you edit, complete, or delete something, its reminder updates automatically.

---

## Your data, your control

Orbit doesn't use the cloud. If you want to move to a new phone or just keep a safe copy, use the backup option in your profile to export everything, then import it whenever you need.
